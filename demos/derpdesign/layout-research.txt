Design decisions:
Four recognizable presets: Classic, Side tabs, Bottom address, Compact sidebar.
Side tabs exposes Left/Right and Top/Bottom; Compact exposes Left/Right. Keep side and address preferences when switching presets. Immediate changes, no Apply/restart step.
Preserve DerpBird's existing 34px horizontal tab row, 40px navigation row, typography, colors, tab menus and active-state treatment. Use green-accent browser diagrams rather than copying another browser's theme.
All full-width address layouts move the entire navigation row together. Suggestions open toward the page, upward for bottom placement.
Compact keeps a visible domain with the full URL on hover. Click or Ctrl+L opens a centered, full-URL editor with suggestions and open-tab switching. Up/Down, Enter, Escape and outside-click are supported. The menu stays in the sidebar's top row.
The settings dialog is scrollable at narrow sizes. Radio inputs retain keyboard semantics. Right-side resizing reverses the pointer delta.

Scope:
This is an interactive design prototype, opt-in through shellView's layoutDesign argument and external DerpDesign conditions. Existing native settings are not replaced. Position preferences are session-only; native persistence, native compositor placement and native keyboard routing still require product integration. The sample article and suggestion/history corpus are deterministic fixtures, not live browsing.

Delivery:
Demo id: derpbird-browser-layouts. Ten Ladybird panes: eight combinations, settings, palette.
layout-declaration.ts is the portable demo definition.
register-layout.ts configures and selects only an explicitly supplied owned headless session.
publish-layout.ts registers the demo inactive in an already-running desktop service; it never launches or selects a demo.
Verification: real Ladybird pane captures, settings switching, palette click/Ctrl+L/keyboard tab switching/Escape/outside click, bottom suggestions, tab context menu, right sidebar drag (240px to 356px). Settings also inspected at 480px. Source tests cover interrupted/replaced resize handles on both sides.
